About

I build companies that solve problems close to home.

I'm Nandhu S. Mahendran, Founder & CEO of four companies based in Ramanathapuram. Blunar Co is the parent company. Zeshin builds software and AI products, Med Blunar builds medical technology, and HodoMate builds technology for travel and tourism.

Blunar Co also runs a digital marketing studio for businesses across Tamil Nadu. I'm an ECE student at Syed Ammal Engineering College, where much of our team and our first products started.
